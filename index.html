How to Play

🎨 The Challenge

Test your knowledge of Norwegian art history! You'll be shown famous Norwegian paintings and need to identify the artist who created them.

🎯 How It Works

  • Look at the painting displayed on the left
  • Choose the correct artist from the four options on the right
  • Get instant feedback on your answer
  • Track your progress with the streak indicator

🏆 Scoring

  • Each correct answer adds to your streak
  • Get 10 in a row for a perfect score!
  • View your results at the end of each round
  • Download certificates for perfect scores

🎭 Categories

Choose from different categories to focus on specific artists or time periods. Use the dropdown menu at the top right to select your preferred category.

📚 Learn More

Click "Painters" to see all artists in the collection, or "Gallery" to browse all paintings. Each artist has a detailed biography and their works.
