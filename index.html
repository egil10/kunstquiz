About Kunstquiz

🎨 The Collection

Kunstquiz features 3,282 paintings from 81 Norwegian artists, making it one of the most comprehensive Norwegian art quizzes available. Our collection spans from the 19th century to contemporary works, covering various movements and styles.

📊 Quiz Categories

  • Full Collection: All 3,282 paintings
  • Popular Painters: Top 10 artists with most works
  • Landscape Painting: 1,745 landscape works
  • Portraits: 584 portrait paintings
  • Women Painters: 259 works by female artists
  • Impressionism: 392 impressionist works
  • Expressionism: 253 expressionist paintings
  • Norwegian Romantic: 765 romantic nationalist works

🎯 How to Play

Select a category, view a painting, and choose the correct artist from four options. Build streaks and learn about Norwegian art history with each answer!

💡 Interesting Facts

  • Data sourced from open Wikimedia and Wikidata APIs
  • Features 33 different art genres including landscape, portrait, and abstract painting
  • Includes 7 major art movements from Impressionism to Contemporary art
  • Collection spans over 200 years of Norwegian art history
  • All images are freely available under open licenses

🛠️ Technical Details

Built with modern web technologies, featuring responsive design for all devices. The quiz uses intelligent artist weighting to ensure fair representation regardless of collection size.
